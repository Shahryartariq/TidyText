About {{ app_name }}

{{ app_name }} fixes the small, annoying problems text picks up when you copy it from one place to another.

Why it exists

Text copied from a PDF breaks at the end of every printed line. Text from a web page brings hidden characters and odd spaces. Text typed with caps lock on needs retyping. Each fix takes seconds here, without installing anything or signing up.

What makes it different

  • Private by design. Your text is cleaned in your browser and never uploaded. There are no accounts{% if not analytics_enabled %}, cookies or trackers{% endif %}.
  • Instant. The result updates as you type or change an option.
  • {{ tools|length }} tools in one place. Fix PDF text, remove duplicates, sort lists, strip HTML, find and replace, count words and more, and combine them freely.
  • Honest about changes. You see exactly how many characters, words and lines were removed.
  • Works everywhere. On phones, tablets and computers, with or without JavaScript.
